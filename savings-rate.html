What result is the calculator targeting?

It finds the estimated minimum savings rate needed to reach your desired portfolio balance at the start of the final age you selected. Contributions occur throughout each working year, and passive income begins at retirement and then grows with inflation.

The result is an illustration, not a recommended savings rate. Real returns, expenses, and life circumstances vary, so you may want to test a higher desired ending balance or more cautious assumptions.
