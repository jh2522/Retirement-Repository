ABOUT THIS TOOL

Retirement planning should feel understandable.

I wanted a retirement calculator that regular people could understand, change, and explore for themselves.

I built this site to make it easier to see how saving, investing, inflation, and spending work together over a lifetime.

I do not expect one calculation to predict the future. The useful part is changing an input, comparing the result, and seeing which choices make the biggest difference for your retirement.
